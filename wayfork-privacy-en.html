King of Forks — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026 · Operator: Jovi Games · Contact: jovigames@proton.me · 한국어

1. Information processed and purposes

King of Forks (the "Game") has no sign-up. The operator does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, phone number or email. Your game progress (hero, gear, pals, records, currencies) is stored only on your device. The Game does not use location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

Friend invites. If you use friend invites, the Game sends the following to the operator's invite server (Amazon Web Services, Seoul region): a SHA-256 hash of a device identifier (the original value is never sent), your invite code, the friend code you entered and the invite relationship, and the times invite stages were reached (first run, chapter clears, first Legendary/Mythic gear). This is used to pay invite rewards and prevent abuse, and is deleted automatically 400 days after the last record. IP addresses are used only in memory for a one-minute rate limit and are not stored. No advertising ID, name or account is sent.

The following services included in the Game process the information below. The processor and overseas recipient is Google LLC (USA); data is sent over the internet while you use the app.

Retention and processing follow the Google Privacy Policy. If you stop using or uninstall the app, further transfers stop.

2. Retention and deletion

Game progress is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in your device settings, and cannot be recovered. Non-consumable purchases (Remove interstitial ads, Pathfinder Fund, etc.) can be recovered with [Restore purchases] in the shop.

3. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of processing of your information, and where GDPR applies you may object, request portability and complain to a supervisory authority. You can manage on-device data as described above and data processed by Google through your Google account settings. To delete invite-server records, email us your invite code (shown on the in-game friend invite screen). Questions are welcome at the email below.

4. Personalized ads

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID and turn off personalized ads in your device's Google ads settings. In regions that require consent, the Game is offered only after a personalized-ads consent screen is in place.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you believe a child's information has been processed, please contact us. Where a country sets a higher age, that age applies.

6. In-app purchases and refunds

Digital items are delivered immediately on purchase, and withdrawal is limited once used. Unused items can be refunded through Google Play within 7 days of purchase, and purchases made by minors without a guardian's consent can be cancelled. Odds for random items (boxes, pal summons) are shown in the in-game [Odds] screen.

7. Security

Communication with external services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Privacy contact

Jovi Games privacy contact: jovigames@proton.me
