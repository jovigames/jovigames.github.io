Starpour — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026 · Operator: Jovi Games · Contact: jovigames@proton.me · 한국어

1. Information processed and purposes

Starpour (the "Game") has no sign-up. The operator does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, phone number or email. Game progress (stage records, star cards, currencies such as gems, stardust and telescope tickets, the Observatory and settings) is stored only on your device. The operator runs a server only for the friend invite feature below. The Game does not use location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

Friend invites: to issue invite codes and to reward both sides when a friend reaches in-game achievements (stages, chapters, first Mythic star), the operator's friend invite server (Amazon Web Services, Seoul region) processes the information below. The device value sent to the server is a SHA-256 hash of a device identifier mixed with the game name; the original value is never sent. The invite text and a Google Play link are handed to the messaging or SMS app you choose, or to your clipboard, and anything sent from there follows that app's policy.

The services below, included in the Game, process the following information. The processor and recipient of the international transfer is Google LLC (USA); data is sent over the internet while you use the app.

Retention and processing by Google services follow the Google Privacy Policy. Friend invite server records are deleted automatically 400 days after the last record. If you stop using or uninstall the app, transfers stop.

2. Retention and deletion

Game progress is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in device settings, and cannot be recovered. Non-consumable purchases (Remove Ads, the Starter Pack’s permanent bonus, the Comeback Gift and Growth Fund membership) can be recovered with [Restore] in the shop. Currency already received is not granted again.

3. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your information and, in GDPR regions, object, request portability and complain to a supervisory authority. Data on your device can be handled as described above and data processed by Google through your Google account settings. To delete friend invite server records, email us with the invite code shown under [Invite Friends] in the Game. Questions are welcome at the email below.

4. Personalised ad consent

Where consent is required, a consent screen appears when the Game starts, and you can change your choice at any time in [More > Settings > Ad privacy settings]. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google ads settings.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child's information has been processed, please let us know. Where a country sets a higher age, that age applies.

6. In-app purchases and refunds

Digital items are delivered immediately on purchase, so withdrawal is limited once used. Unused items may be refunded through Google Play within 7 days of purchase, and purchases made by minors without a guardian's consent may be cancelled.

7. Security

Communication with external services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Privacy contact

Jovi Games privacy contact: jovigames@proton.me
