Effective date: November 1, 2026 · Operator: Jovi Games · Contact: jovigames@proton.me

1. Information we collect

Roll Guard (the "Game") has no account registration and does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, phone number, or email address. Game progress (spirits, currency, records, and similar data) is stored on your device. If you connect Google Play Games, it is also stored in your Google account's storage (Saved Games).

The following third-party services included in the Game may automatically process the information below.

Google AdMob (ads)Advertising ID, device information, IP address (approximate location), ad interactionsShowing and measuring ads, personalized ads (where you have consented)

These services handle information according to the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

2. Retention and deletion

Game progress is stored on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or use [Settings > Reset Data] in the Game. Cloud saves can be deleted through data management in the Google Play Games app. The operator does not store user data on its own servers.

3. Personalized ad consent and opting out

In regions where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, a consent screen appears when the Game starts. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device under Settings > Google > Ads.

4. Children

The Game is not primarily directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

5. In-app purchases and withdrawal

Digital items are delivered immediately after purchase, and the right of withdrawal is limited once they have been used. For unused items, you may request a refund through Google Play within 7 days of purchase. Purchases made by a minor without a legal guardian's consent may be canceled.

Privacy inquiries: jovigames@proton.me
