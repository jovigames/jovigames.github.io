Forgeling — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026 · Operator: Jovi Games · Contact: jovigames@proton.me · 한국어

1. Information processed and purposes

Forgeling (the "Game") has no sign-up. The operator does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, phone number or email. The operator's server stores only the friend-invite data in the table below. Game progress (gear, currencies, spirits, stage records) is stored only on your device. The Game does not use location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

The services below, included in the Game, process the following information. For Google services, the processor and recipient of the international transfer is Google LLC (USA); data is sent over the internet while you use the app.

Operator's server: the data in the table above is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to Amazon Web Services servers in Seoul, Republic of Korea when you start the Game (to create your invite code), use the invite screen or reach a friend step, and server operation is entrusted to Amazon Web Services (AWS Privacy Notice). For users outside Korea, this is a transfer to the Republic of Korea. If you stop using or uninstall the app, transfers stop.

2. Retention and deletion

Game progress is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in device settings, and cannot be recovered. Non-consumable purchases (Remove Ads, Starter Pack, Welcome Back Pack, Growth Fund) can be recovered with [Restore] in the shop. Invite records on the operator's server are deleted automatically 400 days after the last record. If the server stops, the Game keeps working and only invite rewards are delayed.

3. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your information and, in GDPR regions, object, request portability and complain to a supervisory authority. Data on your device can be handled as described above and data processed by Google through your Google account settings. To delete invite records on the operator's server, email us with your invite code. Other questions are welcome at the email below.

4. Personalised ad consent

Where the law requires consent, it is requested before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalised ads in your device's Google ads settings.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child's information has been processed, please let us know. Where a country sets a higher age, that age applies.

6. In-app purchases and refunds

Digital items are delivered immediately on purchase, so withdrawal is limited once used. Unused items may be refunded through Google Play within 7 days of purchase, and purchases made by minors without a guardian's consent may be cancelled.

7. Security

Communication with the operator's server and external services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS), and the operator's server stores device identifiers only as hashes.

8. Privacy contact

Jovi Games privacy contact: jovigames@proton.me
