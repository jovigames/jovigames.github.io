1. Data we process and why

Final Rampart (the "Game") has no sign-up, and the operator does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name or phone number. If you email us, see section 9 for your address and message. Game progress (weapons, currency, records) is stored on your device.

Google Play Games: If this device is signed in to Play Games, the Game uploads your save automatically when the app starts, after battles and when the app goes to the background, even if you never press the Connect button in the Game. It is stored in the app data area of your Google Drive (Saved Games, not visible in your Drive file list). The app receives your Player ID and profile display name from Play Games, uses them only inside the app and does not send them to the operator. If you are not signed in, nothing is uploaded.

Local notifications: Notifications are scheduled on your device only if you turn them on (Sentry Duty full, free crate ready), are not sent to a server, and can be turned off in Game or device settings.

Our relationship with Google: The services below are provided by Google LLC (USA) and its affiliates. The operator does not receive this data directly. Google handles Google Play Billing and Play Games data under its own responsibility, and AdMob data under its advertising terms (how Google uses information from partner sites). Data may be sent over the internet to the USA and other countries while you use the app.

2. Ally invites

The ally invite feature is turned off as of the effective date. It does not appear in the Game, no invite code is created and no invite data is sent. If it is turned on, this policy will first state what data is sent, the processor and the retention period.

3. Retention and deletion

Progress on your device: Kept until you uninstall the app or use Settings > Reset Data. Purchased gems, Monthly Supply and War Pass are lost with it. One-time items like Remove Ads can be recovered with Restore Purchases.

Cloud saves: Kept until you delete them in data management in the Google Play Games app. Reset Data or uninstalling does not delete them.

Device backup: If device backup is on, app data may be included in your Android backup in your Google account and may come back when you reinstall. To remove it fully, also delete this app's backup in your device and Google account backup settings.

Support email: Deleted within 1 year after the request is closed, unless the law requires longer.

Data handled by Google: Retention and processing follow the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your data. Where GDPR applies, you may also object, request portability and complain to a supervisory authority. The operator holds no identifying data, so data on your device and Google account can be handled as described above and in your Google account settings. Send other requests to the email below.

5. Personalized ad consent and withdrawal

Where consent is required, a consent screen appears when the Game starts. To change or withdraw your choice, open Settings in the Game and tap "Change Ad Consent". Your game progress is not erased. Email us below if you need help. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the Google ads menu in device settings. The ad SDK does not start and no ads are requested until the consent step is finished.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child's data was processed, please tell us at the email below. Where a country sets a higher age, that age applies.

7. In-app purchases and refunds

Digital items are delivered instantly. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy and applicable law. Odds for randomized items are shown in the in-game shop.

8. Security

The operator does not store user data itself, and communication with third-party services uses encrypted connections.

9. Privacy contact

If you email us, your address and message are used only to answer and handle the request. Email is stored with the operator's mail provider (Proton).

Jovi Games privacy contact: jovigames@proton.me
