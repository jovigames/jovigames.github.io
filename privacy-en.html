Last-Second Legend — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 9, 2026 · Operator: Jovi Games · Contact: jovigames@proton.me · 한국어

1. Data we process and why

Last-Second Legend ("the Game") has no account registration. The operator does not collect information that directly identifies you (such as name, phone number or email) and does not store user data on its own servers. Game progress (spirits, currencies, records) is stored on your device and, if you link Google Play Games, in your Google account's Saved Games storage.

The third-party services below process the following data. The processor and overseas recipient is Google LLC (United States); data is transmitted over the internet while the app is in use.

Retention and handling follow the Google Privacy Policy. If you stop using or delete the app, further transmission stops.

2. Retention and deletion

Game progress is deleted from your device when you uninstall the app or use Settings > Reset data. Currencies such as gems may be lost with it, so linking a Google account is recommended. Cloud saves can be deleted through data management in the Google Play Games app.

3. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of processing, and in GDPR regions you may also object, request portability and complain to a supervisory authority. Because the operator holds no identifying data, data on your device and Google account can be managed directly as described above and in your Google account settings. Other requests can be sent to the address below.

4. Personalized ads: consent and withdrawal

Where consent is required, a consent screen appears when the Game starts, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google ads settings.

5. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child's data has been processed, please contact us. Where a country sets a higher age, that age applies.

6. In-app purchases

Refunds are handled through Google Play under its refund policy and the consumer laws of your country. Digital items are delivered immediately upon purchase.

7. Security

The operator does not store user data itself, and communication with third-party services uses encrypted connections.

8. Contact

Jovi Games privacy contact: jovigames@proton.me
