Nestborn — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 7, 2026 · Operator: Jovi Games · Contact: jovigames@proton.me · 한국어

1. Information we process and why

Nestborn (the "Game") has no sign-up. The operator does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, phone number or email, and runs no game server. Your progress (creatures, eggs, currencies, codex) and play log (play time, battle results, hatch grades) are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere. Tapping [Copy play log] in Settings copies a one-line summary to your clipboard; it is shared only if you paste it yourself. The Game does not use location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

The services below, included in the Game, process the following information. The current version includes only Google AdMob (rewarded ads); Google Play Billing and Play Games will be added in a later version. The processor and overseas recipient is Google LLC (USA); data is sent over the internet while you use the app.

2. Retention and deletion

Game progress is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in device settings, and cannot be recovered.

3. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your information, and in GDPR regions you may object, request portability or complain to a supervisory authority. Because the operator keeps no user information, you can manage on-device data as described above and Google-processed data in your Google Account settings. Contact us at the email below.

4. Personalized ads consent and withdrawal

We ask for consent before personalized ads where the law requires it. Ads play only when you tap a button to watch one (3 per type per day). The consent form is shown with Google UMP (User Messaging Platform), and you can change your choice any time under [Ad privacy choices] in Settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off personalized ads in your device's Google ads settings.

5. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child's information was processed, email us. Where a country sets a higher age, we follow it.

6. In-app purchases and refunds

The current version has no purchases. If purchases are added: digital items are delivered immediately and withdrawal is limited once used. Unused items can be refunded through Google Play within 7 days of purchase, and purchases by minors without a guardian's consent may be cancelled. Odds for random items are shown in the Game.

7. Security

Connections to outside services use encrypted HTTPS.

8. Privacy contact

Jovi Games privacy contact: jovigames@proton.me
