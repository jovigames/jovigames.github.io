Brickbrave — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 7, 2026 · Operator: Jovi Games · Contact: jovigames@proton.me · 한국어

1. Information processed and purposes

Brickbrave (the "Game") has no sign-up. The operator does not collect information that directly identifies you (name, phone number, email) and runs no server for the Game. Game progress (stage records, heroes and levels, gems, gold, summon count, settings) is stored only on your device. The Game does not use location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

The services below, included in the Game, process the following information. The processor and recipient abroad is Google LLC (USA); data is transmitted over the internet while you use the app.

ServiceInformationPurposeLegal basis (GDPR regions)
Google AdMobAdvertising ID, device information, IP address (approximate location), ad interactions, app diagnosticsShowing and measuring ads, fraud prevention, personalized ads (if you consent)Consent (personalized ads), legitimate interest (fraud prevention, measurement)
Google Play Billing (if purchases are offered)Order number and purchase historyPayment, delivery, restorePerformance of contract

Retention and processing follow the Google Privacy Policy. If you stop using or uninstall the app, transmission stops.

2. Retention and deletion

Game progress is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in device settings, and cannot be recovered.

3. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of processing of your information, and in GDPR regions you may object, request portability and complain to a supervisory authority. Because the operator keeps no user information, on-device data can be handled as above and data processed by Google through your Google account settings. Contact us at the email below.

4. Consent to personalized ads and withdrawal

Where consent is required, a consent screen is shown when the Game starts. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Google ads settings.

5. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you believe a child's information was processed, contact us. Where a country sets a higher age, that age applies.

6. Purchases and refunds

If purchases are offered, digital items are delivered immediately and withdrawal is limited once used. Unused items may be refunded through Google Play within 7 days of purchase where applicable, and purchases by minors without guardian consent may be cancelled under local law.

7. Random items

Hero summon rates by grade are shown in the Game under Summon > Rates.

8. Security

Communication with external services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

9. Contact

Jovi Games privacy contact: jovigames@proton.me
