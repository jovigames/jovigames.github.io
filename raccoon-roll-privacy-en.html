1. Information processed and why

Raccoon Roll (the "Game") has no account registration. The operator does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, phone number or email. The operator's server stores only the information in the table below, which is needed for friend invites and purchase checks. Game progress (districts, currencies, cards, purchase records and similar) and your friend invite code are stored on your device. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, progress is also stored in your Google account's Saved Games storage. The Game does not use the location permission.

2. Google Play Games sign-in and cloud save

When the app starts, Google Play Games tries to sign you in automatically. If you are signed in, the Game saves progress to Saved Games automatically (at app start, periodically during play, when you leave the app, and after a purchase). If the saved copy differs from your device, the Game does not overwrite it and asks which one to use.

In the Game's Settings, [Google Play account] shows the link status and lets you save or load by hand. In the same window, [Unlink in this game] stops auto save and cloud save. Saves already uploaded remain, Google sign-in itself is managed in the Play Games app, and you can link again. You can still play with device storage only, without signing in.

3. Notifications

You only receive notifications if you turn them on. They are scheduled and shown on your device. The operator collects nothing for them and sends nothing to a server. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.

5. Retention and deletion

Operator: Invite and purchase check records on the operator's server are deleted 1 year after the last communication, and IP hashes after 30 days. If the server stops, the Game keeps working and only invite rewards are delayed. If you email us, that email is kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

Your device: Game data is deleted from your device when you delete the app. The Game has no in-app data reset menu. Without a cloud save, currencies such as gems may be lost with it.

Saved Games: Stays in your Google account until you delete it. You can delete it in the data settings of Google Play Games.

AdMob and other data processed by Google: Google sets the retention periods. See how Google retains data and how Google uses data from partner apps.

6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your information and, where GDPR applies, object, request portability, or complain to a supervisory authority. You can manage data on your device and Google account directly as described above and in your Google account settings. To delete records on the operator's server, email the address below with your invite code. Other requests can be sent to the same address.

7. Personalized ad consent

Where consent is required, a consent screen is shown, and you can change your choice under [Ad privacy settings] in the Game's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google ad settings.

8. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. In Korea, processing the personal information of a child under 14 requires a legal guardian's consent. The Game has no age check or guardian consent step, so children under 14 in Korea should not use it. If your country sets a higher age, that age applies. If you believe a child's information was processed, please contact us.

9. In-app purchases and refunds

Digital items are delivered right after purchase. Refunds are requested through Google Play's refund process. Time limits and conditions follow Google Play policy and the law of the country where you live. Purchases by minors without guardian consent can be cancelled as the applicable law allows.

10. Security

Communication with the operator's server and with external services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS), and the operator's server stores device identifiers, purchase tokens and IP addresses only as hashes.
