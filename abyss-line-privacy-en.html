Something's Hooked — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026 · Operator: Jovi Games · Contact: jovigames@proton.me · 한국어

1. Information processed and purposes

Something's Hooked (the "Game") has no sign-up. The operator does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, phone number or email, and runs no game server. Game progress (fish records, currencies, gear) is stored only on your device. The Game does not use location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions.

The services below, included in the Game, process the following information. The processor and recipient of the international transfer is Google LLC (USA); data is sent over the internet while you use the app.

ServiceInformationPurposeLegal basis (GDPR regions)
Google AdMobAdvertising ID, device information, IP address (approximate location), ad interactions, app diagnosticsShowing and measuring ads, fraud prevention, personalised ads (if you consent)Consent (personalised ads), legitimate interest (fraud prevention, measurement)

Retention and processing follow the Google Privacy Policy. If you stop using or uninstall the app, transfers stop.

2. Retention and deletion

Game progress is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in device settings, and cannot be recovered. Non-consumable purchases (Remove Ads, Starter Pack) can be recovered with [Restore purchases] in the shop.

3. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of your information and, in GDPR regions, object, request portability and complain to a supervisory authority. The operator keeps no information about you, so data on your device can be handled as described above and data processed by Google through your Google account settings. Questions are welcome at the email below.

4. Personalised ad consent

Where consent is required, a consent screen appears when the Game starts, and you can change your choice at any time in [Options > Ad privacy settings]. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google ads settings.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child's information has been processed, please let us know. Where a country sets a higher age, that age applies.

6. In-app purchases and refunds

Digital items are delivered immediately on purchase, so withdrawal is limited once used. Unused items may be refunded through Google Play within 7 days of purchase, and purchases made by minors without a guardian's consent may be cancelled.

7. Security

Communication with external services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Privacy contact

Jovi Games privacy contact: jovigames@proton.me
